\section{Supplementary Replacement Audits}
\label{sec:supp_replacement}

This appendix retains completeness experiments that are useful for auditing the
scope of LUT substitution but are not part of the semantic-addressability
claim.

\subsection{Grouped Binary-Pattern Projection LUT}

The compact response-prototype address cannot uniquely recover every projection
current when all four attention projections are replaced together. For this
boundary experiment, a binary input $\mathbf x$ is partitioned into $M$ groups
of $g$ bits. For group $m$ and binary pattern $p$, we precompute
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal T_m[p]=\mathbf W_m\mathbf b(p),
\end{equation}
and reconstruct the projection as
\begin{equation}
  \hat{\mathbf y}=\mathbf b_{\mathrm{proj}}+
  \sum_{m=1}^{M}\mathcal T_m[p_m(\mathbf x)].
\end{equation}
The diagnostic evaluates $g\in\{2,4,8\}$ without training. The hook version
still executes the original convolution before substituting its output, so the
experiment tests fidelity rather than measured runtime removal.

\subsection{Native Affine, BN, and LIF Transition LUTs}

The stricter native audit replaces each frozen Conv1d/Conv2d/Linear module with
a scalar lookup-and-accumulate operator, folds paired inference BatchNorm
parameters into the affine tables, and replaces each MultiStepLIF update with a
finite transition table over membrane and input states. Integer-valued LIF
inputs retain exact integer addresses to avoid recurrent clipping introduced by
decoding them through a floating-point grid.

Pooling, residual addition, SSA products/gating, and global or temporal
reductions remain ordinary arithmetic. The audit therefore establishes
operator-class fidelity, not complete-network replacement.

\subsection{Historical All-Affine Output Substitution}

Before the native audit, a hook-level study substituted Q/K/V, MLP,
patch-embedding, classifier, and cumulative affine outputs. Q/K/V incurred a
0.45-point drop; MLP, patch embedding, and classifier changed accuracy by
$-0.05$, $+0.11$, and $-0.01$ points under the clean-minus-LUT convention.
The cumulative 32-operator row produced a no-loss fluctuation. Its FP32 table
values occupied approximately 242.4 MiB, 9.47 times the original weight-value
count. These rows are retained as historical fidelity evidence, not as the
compact method or a storage claim.
