\begin{abstract}
Spiking Transformers replace dense floating-point attention with event-driven
operations, but it remains unclear whether their binary query--key states form
reliable lookup-table (LUT) addresses. We study this question in QKFormer with
a staged, negative-controlled audit that separates address feasibility,
held-out response reconstruction, support-aware fallback, and downstream
adapter utility. Q/K-addressed prototypes reduce held-out response MSE relative
to a global mean by $3.71\%\pm0.004$ on CIFAR-10 and $4.42\%\pm0.004$ on
CIFAR-100 across randomized calibration subsets. Coarse token/channel LUTs
reach only $2.79\%$ and $3.42\%$, while shuffling the address--prototype
alignment degrades MSE by $17.83\%$ and $15.82\%$, respectively. Across three
independently trained single-step CIFAR-10 checkpoints, correct-address
reductions range from $3.76\%$ to $5.94\%$ and exceed token/channel controls in
every case. To address the dimensionality objection against monolithic Q/K
tables, we first audit support-aware hierarchical backoff and then introduce a
subspace-decoupled residual LUT that composes smaller token/channel, Q/gate, and
K subtables. On CIFAR-100, the compact token/channel+Q/gate subspace LUT beats
token/channel lookup for all calibration seeds from 8 to 512 batches using only
$8.83\%$ of full-address supported entries; adding the K subtable uses about
$20.2\%$ entries and retains $93.6\%$--$105.9\%$ of the full-address
reconstruction gain. On CIFAR-100 $T=4$, a deterministic threshold fitted only
on a disjoint train partition raises mean Acc@1 from $81.22\%$ to $81.73\%$
across three adapter seeds; matched shuffled and global controls reach
$81.62\%$ and $81.46\%$. All aligned runs exceed the saved $81.23\%$ QKFormer
checkpoint, while a two-epoch diagnostic reaches $82.02\%$. The accuracy result
is checkpoint-conditional pending independent-backbone replication. Thus
QK-LUTFormer establishes reproducible, interpretable Q/K lookup-addressability
and calibrated selective utility while explicitly avoiding unsupported energy,
latency, or production full-LUT claims.
\end{abstract}
