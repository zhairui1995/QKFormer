\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Spiking Transformer attention.}
Spikformer introduced spike-form self-attention for directly trained spiking
Transformers~\cite{zhou2023spikformer}. Spike-driven Transformer removes
floating-point multiplication from spike-form attention
~\cite{yao2023spikedriven}, SpikingResformer develops dual spike
self-attention~\cite{shi2024spikingresformer}, and QKFormer uses hierarchical
binary token/channel Q-K attention~\cite{zhou2024qkformer}. These works design
accurate spiking backbones. QK-LUTFormer asks a different question: whether
their internal discrete states are reproducible response addresses under
matched negative controls.

\paragraph{LUT and quantized arithmetic inference.}
MADDNESS and LUT-NN replace dense arithmetic with centroid- or
code-indexed table access~\cite{blalock2021maddness,tang2023lutnn}.
Transformer lookup modules such as LookupFFN and MemoryFormer similarly target
feed-forward computation~\cite{zeng2023lookupffn,wang2024memoryformer}. Their
main objective is arithmetic substitution. Our central test is instead whether
an architecture's existing Q/K spike state carries address-specific response
information, and whether that information remains valid after thresholded
dynamics.

\paragraph{Compositional codebooks and associative memory.}
Product-Key Memory factorizes retrieval into compositional
subkeys~\cite{lample2019large}; product and additive quantization reconstruct
vectors from multiple lower-dimensional codebooks
~\cite{jegou2011product,babenko2014additive}. QK-LUTFormer shares the idea of
compositional lookup but does not learn an arbitrary partition of a continuous
embedding. Its subtables follow QKFormer semantics---token/channel, Q/gate,
and K---and store zero-mean conditional response residuals with explicit
support thresholds and shuffled-subspace controls.

\paragraph{SNN dynamics calibration and conversion.}
ANN-to-SNN conversion must preserve activation statistics across temporal
threshold dynamics, and Transformer conversion is additionally complicated by
continuous query--key interactions and normalization. SpikedAttention is one
example of addressing this conversion problem~\cite{hwang2024spikedattention}.
We begin from a natively spiking model rather than converting an ANN. TCSLU
uses moment and time/channel calibration for a narrower purpose: restoring the
dynamic current contract after semantic lookup, before the original BN/LIF
path.
